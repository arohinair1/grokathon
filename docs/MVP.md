# MVP / Initial Experiment

> **Status: FOUNDER-APPROVED PREVIEW.** This scope is a product decision (DEC-001, DEC-002),
> not a conclusion from `docs/RESEARCH.md`. Research synthesis has not been written.

Build a testable Ann Arbor marketplace for parking and storage. Listings are demo data
until a later milestone. Requests, when they exist, are nonbinding. No payments, paid
reservations, or production booking commitments until the founder approves live operations.

The application code lives in `web/`. Milestone 1 is the visual shell, homepage, and
local demo catalog. Later milestones add Supabase, accounts, host tools, and requests.

## Assumption(s) being tested

Not restated from unfinished research. The founder chose to build a preview and judge it in use.

## Experiment design

A public preview with labeled demo inventory, then accounts, host listings, and nonbinding requests. No live marketplace operations until a later approval.

## Target market and segment

Ann Arbor first. Renters looking for parking or storage, and local property owners who might list a space. The data model keeps a city field so other college towns can be added later.

## In scope

Browse, search, and filter demo listings. Listing detail. Later: accounts, host listing management, availability, and nonbinding booking requests.

## Explicitly out of scope

Payments, paid reservations, production booking commitments, maps, Stripe, automatic switching of one space between uses, and treating unfinished research as requirements.
