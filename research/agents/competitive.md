# Specialist: Competitive Landscape (`competitive`, prefix `CL`)

## Mission

Map who already serves (or partially serves) residential parking/storage demand in
college towns, and how well. Determine how much of the project hypothesis is already
covered by existing products or informal alternatives.

## Scope

Direct and adjacent competitors, including but **not limited to**:
- Peer-to-peer storage/parking marketplaces (e.g., Neighbor, Spacer, Prked).
- Parking aggregators/reservation platforms (e.g., SpotHero, ParkWhiz).
- Student-storage services (pickup/dropoff and self-storage aimed at students), including
  university-affiliated or university-endorsed programs.
- Event-parking marketplaces and event-specific parking apps.
- Traditional self-storage and commercial parking operators near campuses.
- Informal alternatives: homeowners selling game-day parking directly, classified ads,
  Facebook groups/Marketplace, Craigslist, subleases, friends/family, leaving items with
  landlords.

The names above are starting points, not a complete list. **You must actively search for
competitors we have not named** (other countries' equivalents, defunct startups, regional
players, campus-specific services) and report what you found and how you searched.

## For each significant competitor, establish

- What it actually offers (product, inventory type, booking model) — from its own pages.
- Positioning and target customers.
- Pricing and fee/commission structure (host side and renter side), with date observed.
- Host and renter workflows (listing, booking, access, payment, disputes).
- Insurance/protection offered.
- Geographic coverage — in particular, observable presence in college towns, and in Ann
  Arbor specifically (e.g., listings visible in search).
- Evidence of traction (listing counts visible, funding, reviews) — labeled carefully.
- Strengths, weaknesses, and user complaints (reviews, forums).
- Overlap with our hypothesis: which use cases does it already serve? Does it handle
  recurring semester parking, event parking, and storage on the same inventory?
- Defunct competitors: what happened and why, if discoverable.

## Questions to keep asking

- Does anyone already do calendar- or event-aware residential inventory? How?
- How easily could an incumbent add the features that might differentiate us?
- Are informal solutions good enough that a marketplace adds little?

## Output

`research/reports/competitive.md`, plus a comparison table (competitor × dimensions) in the
report. Mark cells you could not verify as "unknown", not blank guesses.
