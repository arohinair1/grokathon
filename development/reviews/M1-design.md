# Verdict: APPROVE WITH NONBLOCKING ISSUES

## Visual direction

College Space uses a warm, editorial marketplace direction: cream surfaces, dark green accents, display serif headings, restrained borders, and large photography. The hierarchy is clear and the result feels considered rather than like a generic dashboard. Search is prominent, while the listing detail keeps the preview-only request state explicit.

## Blocking issues

None.

## Nonblocking issues

- The storage listing photograph reads more as a locksmith or workshop image than as the actual garage being offered. A future photography pass should show the space itself first so renters can understand the inventory immediately.
- The desktop search form's date guidance is small and crowded beside the primary button. Give that note a little more room or move it below the date controls in a later polish pass.
- The listing detail's request-status card is visually sparse relative to the large image and open page area. Its copy is correct, but the composition could feel more balanced at wide widths.
- The supplied `search-results.png` shows the URL being entered over the homepage rather than the rendered results area, so the results-page composition cannot be judged from that image. Demo labeling is nevertheless present in the listing card component, and the detail screenshot visibly carries a `DEMO` label.

## Viewport coverage

Yes. I could see the desktop homepage and listing detail, plus the homepage at about 390px wide. The mobile search form stacks cleanly, remains readable, and shows no visible horizontal overflow. The rendered desktop search-results area itself was not visible in the supplied capture.
